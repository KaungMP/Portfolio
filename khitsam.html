Open Call for Contributors

We are looking for passionate individuals to be a part of our Khit Sam Network as a contributor, in our vision towards creating a digital platform for strong-opinionated articles and a network of youth community.
Currently, we are opening this exciting opportunity to undergraduates, graduates, and young professionals across Myanmar who are passionate in research and writing quality content related to either Governance, Environment, or Gender Equality to have your voice heard on important issues in the country.
Grab this opportunity by applying to be a part of our amazing team and help drive Khit Sam in the Movement.


About Khit Sam

The term “Khit Sam” comes from a new form of writing that some Rangoon University students wrote and these works are called modern because of their exploratory and experimental approach to the creation of Burmese literature. Modern literature was not started as a movement but has become a movement through the experimental work of young writers who are well versed in Burmese and world literature.
With this movement in mind, we want Khit Sam as a digital publication platform where youths can portray their opinions and fact-based articles regarding Governance, Environment, and Gender Equality in real-time. Khit Sam will be delivered in both English and Burmese languages for all-inclusiveness.
Khit Sam will feature articles that will be published digitally on our website and posted on our social media platforms. After the selection process, selected contributors will have a series of writing training and mentorship from the editorial board and external parties. Upon completion, the contributor will also be given the allowance from the Movement team.
In this Phase, we are looking to publish articles across three broad themes and articles can cover a range of topics such as the following examples but they are not limited to them:
  • Governance
    • Youth and Municipal
    • Administration Response to COVID-19
    • Young Ward Administrators
  • Environment
    • Challenges of Managing COVID-19-related Waste
    • Efforts in Wasting Less Plastic
    • Marine Litter
    • Beat Air Pollution
  • Gender Equality
    • Gender Rights
    • Support of Men for Women Rights
    • Cultural Norms and Gender
    • Gender Inclusion
    • Gender Marginalization

Role Descriptions

  • Research
    • Develop ideas on topics related to Governance, Environment, and Gender Equality in the country.
    • Brainstorm with the editorial board to conceptualize ideas and content to be published.
    • Possess reading competency and eager to search for relevant information.
